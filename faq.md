Below are answers to the most common questions about the Summer School on Machine Learning for Electron Microscopy.

---

## Registration & Participation

### **Do I need to pay a registration fee?**
No. Participation in the Summer School is free of charge for both on‑site and online attendees.

### **Are travel, accommodation, or transport costs covered?**
Participants are responsible for covering their own travel, accommodation, and transportation expenses.

### **Can I switch between on‑site and online participation?**
Yes. If you need to switch from on‑site to online participation, please notify the organizers **before June 10** so we can ensure you receive the correct access information.

### **Can I register after the deadline?**
Late registration may be possible:
- **On‑site:** Email us after June 10; lunch availability cannot be guaranteed.
- **Online:** Email us after June 17 to request access.

---

## Event Format

### **Can I participate online?**
Yes. The Summer School can be attended online via Zoom. All registered online participants will receive the connection details before the event.

### **Will sessions be recorded?**
Yes. All sessions will be recorded, processed, and made available to participants approximately **three weeks after the program concludes**. A link will be shared with all attendees once ready.

### **Do I need prior experience in machine learning or electron microscopy?**
No prior ML experience is required. Basic familiarity with electron microscopy is helpful but not mandatory.

## Hands‑On Sessions

### **Do I need to bring my own laptop?**
Yes. On‑site participants should bring a laptop for hands‑on sessions.

### **What software do I need?**
A modern web browser is sufficient. All notebooks can be run in **Google Colab** or a local Python environment.

### **Will datasets and notebooks be provided?**
Yes. All required materials will be supplied during the school.

---

## Venue & Logistics

### **Where is the event located?**
At the **Institute for Advanced Materials & Manufacturing (IAMM)**, University of Tennessee Knoxville.

### **Is lunch provided?**
Lunch and coffee are provided for registered on‑site participants.  
For late on‑site registrations, lunch availability may be limited.

### **Is parking available?**
Yes. Free parking is available at the IAMM building for all on‑site participants. Detailed directions and the exact parking location are provided on the [Venue page](https://kaliningroup.github.io/summer_school/getting-here/).

---

## Program & Content

### **What topics will be covered?**
The school covers:
- STEM imaging and data interpretation  
- Diffraction and 4D‑STEM  
- Spectroscopy (EDS/EELS)  
- Machine learning workflows for microscopy  
- Autonomous and real‑time analytics  

### **Will there be posters or project submissions?**
No. The school consists of lectures, demonstrations, and hands‑on sessions only.
