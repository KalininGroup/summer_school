# 🏛️ Venue & Directions

The Summer School will take place at the  
**University of Tennessee Institute for Advanced Materials & Manufacturing (IAMM)**  
**2641 Osprey Vista Way, Knoxville, TN 37920**

No parking pass is required. Free street parking is available throughout the UT Research Park.
